# unhide

Shows you again in a puff of smoke.

| Syntax | Console | In game | Minimum level | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `unhide` | No | Yes | GameMaster | Game |

```text
.unhide
```

In game only. You are visible again to the players, with a puff of smoke and its sound, and you read
`You are visible again.`. Already visible, you read `You are already that way.`.
