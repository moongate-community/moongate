# echo

Prints its arguments back to you.

| Syntax | Console | In game | Minimum level | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `echo <text>` (alias `e`) | Yes | Yes | Regular | Every role |

```text
echo hello world
e hello world
```

Both forms print `hello world`. With no arguments, the command prints a blank line.
