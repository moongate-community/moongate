# hide

Hides you in a puff of smoke.

| Syntax | Console | In game | Minimum level | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hide` | No | Yes | GameMaster | Game |

```text
.hide
```

In game only. You are hidden: the players do not see you, and neither do the staff of a lower level than yours; a
puff of smoke and its sound mark the place, and you read `You are hidden: players cannot see you.`. Your steps do
not show you, as those of a staff member never do; speaking does not either. Already hidden, you read
`You are already that way.` and nothing else happens.

It is the same hiding as the Hiding skill, kept until you `unhide`. A game master that snoops needs no skill:
see [Snooping](../scripting/shipped-scripts.md#snoopinglua).
